# PEDIATRIC DENTISTRY (Apostol Kids)

Every child receives a special gift after their appointment! A children's playroom is available.

### Treatment without pain and fear:
- **Sandman Micro Dentistry:** Air-abrasion treatment, removing plaque and cavities without drilling, with zero contact with the enamel.
- **Sleep Dentistry (Deep Sedation / Sevoran Anesthesia):** Sevoran gas is safe and non-allergenic. The child falls asleep in seconds, and treatment proceeds without stress. Quick awakening; you can go home in 30 minutes. A blood test and ECG are required beforehand. Strictly on an empty stomach (no food or drink 3 hours prior). Contraindications: ARVI, otitis, epilepsy, traumatic brain injuries.
