# SERVICES (ADULT DEPARTMENT)

### Treatment of teeth (therapy)
Fillings and restorations. Treatment is done under a microscope to preserve as much healthy tissue as possible.

### Hygiene and gum treatment
Professional teeth cleaning with AIR FLOW and ultrasound, polishing and fluoridation. Vector therapy for gum (periodontal) treatment.

### Teeth whitening
In-clinic whitening, whitening of a single tooth, endo-whitening (from inside the tooth) and professional home whitening with Opalescence trays.

### Implantation
Two methods:
- **Classic (multi-stage) implantation:** when at least 3 months have passed since the tooth was removed.
- **Express implantation:** the implant is placed on the same day the tooth is removed.

Implant systems: Straumann (Switzerland), Osstem (South Korea) and Neodent. The implantologist and the prosthodontist choose the system together with the patient, based on a CT scan.

### Prosthetics, veneers and aesthetic dentistry
Crowns, prostheses and veneers, made in the clinic's own dental laboratory.

### Surgery
Tooth extraction and surgical treatment of gums.

### Orthodontics (bite correction)
- Braces: metal, ceramic, sapphire and lingual (on the inner side of the teeth).
- Clear aligners, including Invisalign®.
- Mini-implants, removable appliances, retainers.

The orthodontist chooses the appliance after diagnostics.

# PEDIATRIC DENTISTRY (Apostol Kids)

Treatment of baby teeth, fissure sealing, and Sandman micro-dentistry: air-abrasion cleaning that removes plaque and cavities without a drill.

### Treatment of children under sedation
- **Light sedation:** nitrous oxide.
- **Deep sedation:** Sevoran gas. The child falls asleep within seconds and treatment goes without stress. The child can go home about 30 minutes after the procedure.

Sedation helps the child sleep but does not relieve pain, so local anesthesia is still used.

### How to prepare a child for deep sedation
- The child must be healthy on the day of treatment.
- Consultation with the anesthesiologist beforehand.
- A general blood test and an ECG are required.
- No food or drink for 3 hours before the procedure.
- Tell the doctor about any chronic or past illnesses and any medicines the child takes.

Contraindications: acute respiratory infection (ARVI), otitis, epilepsy, raised intracranial pressure, intolerance to sedatives, head injury, mental disorders.

### How to pay for dental treatment with pension savings (ENPF)
1. Open a special account in the Otbasy Bank app (HCSBK24 / ЖССБ24) or through video banking.
2. Apply for a one-time pension payout on enpf-otbasy.kz and sign it with your digital signature (ECP).
3. ENPF transfers the money to your special account at Otbasy Bank within 5 working days.
4. When the money has arrived, come to the clinic for diagnostics.
5. After diagnostics, apply to your local polyclinic (the medical organization you are attached to) for a medical commission opinion. Attach a copy of your ID.
6. The commission decides within 5 working days: a referral for treatment or a refusal.
7. The commission secretary issues the opinion within 2 working days.
8. Come to the clinic to sign the treatment contract. It includes the clinic's license, the type of service, the diagnosis from the commission's opinion and the clinic's details.
9. Upload the commission's opinion and the contract as PDF files to enpf-otbasy.kz.
10. Otbasy Bank checks the documents within 5 working days and transfers the money to the clinic.
